Application Statuses

Define workflow steps and their sub-statuses. Status changes are triggered by transmit actions or decision widgets. Each step groups related statuses together.

info

A status change happens either when a user transmits (forwards the application) or makes a decision in the approval widget. Check InOrderStatus to enforce sequential progression. Any change triggers a new version of the workflow.
